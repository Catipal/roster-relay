# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Pair with your BPS Relay by scanning the code on its Server page, or by pasting its text.
- See the allowance, what is left of the day's limit, and recent payments.
- Pay an address or a BPS name within the limits set on the relay, confirmed with your PIN.
- Receive at an address of the allowance wallet, shown as a QR code.
- Reach the relay through three relays when it is listed as one, or straight
  to it on the same network or through a forwarded port.
- Pair before the allowance wallet exists; the app says it cannot pay yet.
- The device key is kept in the Android Keystore or the desktop's keyring.
