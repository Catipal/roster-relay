BPS Companion pays and receives BPS without holding any coin keys.

Your own BPS Relay at home keeps a small allowance wallet and signs for this phone, within limits only the relay can change:
* so much per payment and so much a day, set when you pair the phone;
* a PIN, checked by the relay, that locks the phone after five wrong tries;
* the phone can be unpaired from the relay at any time.

The phone holds only a key of its own, kept in the Android Keystore, which can spend nothing by itself. It keeps no blockchain and uses the network only while the app is open.

When your relay is listed as a relay, the app reaches it through three other relays, so none of them, and nobody watching your home connection, learns where the phone is. Otherwise it connects straight to your relay: on the same Wi-Fi, or from anywhere through a forwarded port.

Pair by scanning the code your BPS Relay shows (Server page, Phone companion), or paste its text. Pay an address or a BPS name, scan a payment code, receive, and see your recent activity.

You need a computer running BPS Relay with the phone companion turned on.
